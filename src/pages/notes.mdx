## A simple PD controller

For a joint with target position $q_d$ and measured position $q$, an example control law is

$$
\tau = K_p(q_d - q) - K_d\dot{q}.
$$

Here, $K_p$ is the proportional gain and $K_d$ is the derivative gain.
